Privacy, Terms & Support

1. Local-First Storage & Privacy

By default, BenchLedger stores your repair tickets, supplies, and photos locally inside your browser (using LocalStorage and IndexedDB). If you choose to sign in, your data is synced to an isolated, private Google Firebase Firestore collection accessible only to your authenticated UID.

2. Data Deletion & GDPR Control

You can export your data to JSON/CSV at any time. If you have created a Cloud Sync account, you can permanently erase your cloud data and delete your account immediately using the Delete Account & Cloud Data button inside the left sidebar menu.

3. Terms of Service & Disclaimer

BenchLedger is provided "as-is" without warranty of any kind. Always maintain regular local JSON backups of critical financial or workshop records. Clearing your browser cache while in Local Mode without a JSON backup or Cloud Sync will remove local browser data.
